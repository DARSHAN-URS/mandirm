Privacy Policy

Mandirm ("Mandiram") App & Services • Effective Date: October 8, 2026

Welcome to Mandirm ("we", "our", or "us"). We are dedicated to providing a sacred, reliable, and spiritually enriching experience while honoring your privacy. This Privacy Policy details how we collect, use, disclose, and protect your information when you use our mobile application (Mandirm) and associated online services.

1. Information We Collect

A. Information You Provide to Us

  • Account & Authentication: Mobile phone number, email address, and profile name used for OTP-based secure sign-in.
  • Spiritual & Ritual Details: Devotee name, Gotra, birth details (date, time, and place of birth for astrological consultations and horoscope generation), and family member names for Puja Sankalp offerings.
  • Orders & Offerings: Delivery address for sanctified Prasad and puja items, transaction order summaries, and recipient names.

B. Device & Hardware Permissions

We request only the permissions strictly required to deliver app features:

  • CAMERA Required strictly for participating in live video consultations with certified Vedic Astrologers. We do not access or record your camera without your explicit call action.
  • MICROPHONE / RECORD_AUDIO Required for real-time 1-on-1 audio and video astrology consultations.
  • INTERNET & NETWORK STATE Required to access live temple Darshan feeds, puja updates, and sync devotional data.
  • NOTIFICATIONS Used to alert you about puja schedules, daily panchang, muhurat reminders, and consultation call invites.

2. Payment & Financial Data

All financial transactions (Puja bookings, Chadhawa, consultation bookings, and shop purchases) are processed through secure, RBI-authorized payment gateways (e.g., Razorpay). Mandirm never stores or accesses your credit/debit card numbers, CVVs, UPI PINs, or net banking passwords.

3. Third-Party Service Providers

To provide high-quality services, we partner with industry-standard, secure infrastructure providers:

  • Supabase: Secure cloud database, encrypted storage, and user authentication.
  • Agora RTC: Real-time encrypted audio and video streaming for astrologer consultations.
  • Razorpay: Encrypted payment processing conforming to PCI-DSS compliance standards.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging: Secure push notifications.

4. How We Use Your Information

  • To perform personalized Vedic Pujas and rituals with your designated Sankalp.
  • To connect you with certified astrologers via audio and video calls.
  • To safely deliver sacred Prasad and spiritual goods to your address.
  • To provide accurate Hindu Panchang, Tithi, and astrological horoscope calculations.
  • To maintain app security, prevent fraud, and comply with legal requirements.

5. Data Retention & Account Deletion

We retain your profile and order history only as long as necessary to fulfill religious services and account management.

Account & Data Deletion Rights: You have the absolute right to request the deletion of your account and associated personal data at any time. To request deletion:

  • Email our data privacy officer at support@mandiram.com with the subject line "Account Deletion Request" along with your registered phone number or email address.
  • Your account and associated personal records will be permanently deleted within 7 business days.

6. Security of Your Data

We implement industry-standard HTTPS encryption (TLS 1.3), access controls, and hashed authentication tokens to protect your personal and spiritual data from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.

7. Children's Privacy

Our app does not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13 years of age. Devotional services for minors must be requested and managed by a parent or legal guardian.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
