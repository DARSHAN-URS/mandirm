Standards Against Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE)

Mandirm ("Mandiram") • Comprehensive Child Safety & Protection Standards

Zero Tolerance Policy: Mandirm strictly prohibits any form of child sexual abuse, sexual exploitation, predatory behavior, or the creation, storage, transmission, or facilitation of Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM).

At Mandirm, our commitment to maintaining a sacred, wholesome, and safe environment is absolute. Protecting children from exploitation and harm is our utmost responsibility. We enforce a zero-tolerance policy against Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE) and Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM) across all services, communication channels, and community features of our platform.

1. Strictly Prohibited Activities

Users, devotees, astrologers, vendors, and staff are strictly prohibited from engaging in any of the following activities on or through the Mandirm application:

  • Uploading, transmitting, publishing, linking to, or requesting any images, videos, audio, text, or depictions containing Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM).
  • Online grooming, soliciting, enticing, or attempting to establish contact with minors for sexual or exploitative purposes.
  • Facilitating, organizing, or promoting child sexual exploitation or abuse in any form, including live video or audio sessions.
  • Using astrology consultation tools, chat rooms, or live streams to engage in sexual conversations involving minors or targeted at minors.
  • Sharing or trading contact details, third-party links, or digital media that promote or facilitate harm against children.

2. Enforcement and Immediate Consequences

Any violation of these standards will trigger swift and definitive action:

  • Immediate Account Termination: Offending user and astrologer accounts are immediately banned and permanently disabled without prior notice.
  • Content Removal: Any associated content, messages, or materials are immediately isolated and permanently removed from our platform.
  • Law Enforcement Reporting: In compliance with international and national child safety mandates (including the POCSO Act and Indian IT Rules), violations will be reported directly to law enforcement authorities and international reporting bodies such as the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and relevant cybercrime authorities.

3. Proactive Monitoring & Consultation Safety

To uphold these standards across all interactive features (such as 1-on-1 astrologer calls and chat):

  • All astrologers undergo rigorous identity verification and onboarding reviews.
  • Automated filters and manual moderation review flags, inappropriate behavior reports, and complaints in real-time.
  • Users and astrologers can instantly end any consultation and block or report suspicious users through in-app safety controls.

4. How to Report Violations

If you observe, suspect, or encounter any activity that violates these child protection standards, please report it immediately through any of the following channels:

5. Cooperation with Law Enforcement

Mandirm cooperates fully with national and international law enforcement agencies, cybercrime investigators, and child welfare authorities. We preserve evidence according to legal requirements and support criminal investigations to ensure perpetrators of child exploitation are prosecuted.
